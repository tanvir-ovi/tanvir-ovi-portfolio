brain-surface.bin — attribution and license

Derived from "Brain for Blender" by Anderson M. Winkler:
https://brainder.org/research/brain-for-blender/

MRI-derived pial surfaces of both hemispheres, left and right cerebellar
cortex, and brainstem. Geometry reduced with meshoptimizer, oriented for
the portfolio, with curvature shading added. No generated anatomy.

Original and derivative geometry: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Binary format (little endian): "BRN1" magic (4 bytes), uint32 vertex count,
uint32 index count, uint32 vertex stride (16). Each vertex holds four
float32 values: x, y, z, sulcal shade. Followed by uint32 triangle indices.
